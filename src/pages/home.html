01

Open by default

Source you can read, licences you can live with, and no surprise paywall later.

02

Your data stays yours

Plain formats, exportable state, and designs that work with encryption instead of around it.

03

Approachable first

If you need a weekend to get started, we failed. One command, clear docs, honest defaults.

About

Hi, I'm Bharat.

I build platforms for a living, and open tools for everyone else. b32n is where those tools live: software that's free to use, simple to self-host, and designed so the data it touches stays under your control.

It starts as a personal studio. The goal is bigger: make open source feel approachable for people who never thought it was for them. That's also why I made Bit, a friendly course in computer science essentials for vibe coders.
