How I build

Good software is assembled, not handed down. Every project I make starts from the same three rules.

01

Open by default

Source you can read, licences you can live with, and no surprise paywall later.

02

Your data stays yours

Plain formats, exportable state, and designs that work with encryption instead of around it.

03

Approachable first

If you need a weekend to get started, I've failed. One command, clear docs, honest defaults.

About

Hi, I'm Bharat.

By day I'm Head of Platform at MAJORITY in Stockholm. I look after the whole platform: DevOps, SRE, cybersecurity, IT operations and InfoSec, plus the compliance that comes with a regulated fintech, like SOC 2 and PCI DSS. I also keep a close eye on what all that infrastructure costs, and on making it cost less.

I built and lead an AI-native platform team there, run the company's AI infrastructure, and look after its crypto work, from policy through to smart contracts. Getting here took 12+ years of hands-on engineering: software developer, then senior developer and team lead, then platform engineer, then Head of Platform.

What I care about hasn't changed much along the way: platforms that are reliable, secure and cost-efficient, AI infrastructure that actually ships, and compliance that works as an advantage instead of a drag.

b32n is what I do with the rest of my time. It's just me, building open tools that are free to use, simple to self-host, and designed so your data stays under your control. Spindle and Mindmaps are the first. I also made Bit, a friendly course in computer science essentials for vibe coders, because open source should feel approachable for people who never thought it was for them.

Always happy to talk platform leadership, AI infrastructure, security and compliance at scale, or open source.
